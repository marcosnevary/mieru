# mieru

見える, "to be visible": learn Japanese vocabulary by linking each word directly to an image instead of to its English translation.

## Who it is for

Anki users learning Japanese who want picture cards without assembling furigana, audio and images by hand.

## What it does

- Type an English word; choose among the Japanese words the dictionary offers, each with its reading and meanings.
- The card's front shows the Japanese word with furigana and plays a native recording (or synthesized speech when none exists).
- Choose the picture that represents the word; it is the whole answer on the back. When the word itself makes a poor picture search (nice to meet you), search pictures for a more concrete term (handshake) without losing the chosen Japanese word.
- Type a list of words (dog, cat, bank) and go through them one by one, choosing the Japanese word and picture for each; every word is loaded up front, and you can move back and forth or skip any you don't want.

## Out of scope for now

- Japanese → picture cards for words typed in Japanese.
- Example sentences, pitch accent, kanji breakdowns.
- Picture sources other than Pixabay.

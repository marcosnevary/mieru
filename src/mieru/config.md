Pictures for the back of the card come from Pixabay and Pexels. Each needs its own free API key; set at least one. With both, results from both appear side by side.

**pixabay_api_key**: illustrations and photos, strongest for objects.

1. Create a free account at [pixabay.com](https://pixabay.com/accounts/register/).
2. Open [pixabay.com/api/docs](https://pixabay.com/api/docs/) while logged in; your key is shown under "Parameters › key".
3. Paste it between the quotes and press OK.

**pexels_api_key**: photos, strongest for people and situations (greetings, actions, feelings).

1. Create a free account at [pexels.com](https://www.pexels.com/join/).
2. Open [pexels.com/api](https://www.pexels.com/api/) and request a key.
3. Paste it between the quotes and press OK.
